# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Objective
To perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data to identify sales patterns, customer demographics, product category performance, and useful business insights.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset shape, columns, and data types
- Missing value analysis
- Mean, median, mode, and standard deviation
- Monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Age and gender analysis
- Product category sales analysis
- Correlation heatmap
- Transaction analysis by product category
- Sales by gender and age group
- Top 10 customers by sales

## Key Insights
- Total sales generated: 456,000
- Electronics is the best-selling category with sales of 156,905.
- Female customers contributed the highest sales of 232,840.
- The 46–60 age group generated the highest sales of 147,875.
- CUST015 is the top customer with sales of 2,000.

## Actionable Recommendations
1. Focus marketing campaigns and promotions on the Electronics category.
2. Develop targeted offers based on customer gender and age-group sales patterns.
3. Identify and reward high-value customers to encourage repeat purchases.

## Conclusion
The analysis provides useful insights into sales trends, customer demographics, and product category performance. These findings can help businesses improve marketing strategies and make better data-driven decisions.

## Dataset
Public retail sales dataset used for educational and analytical purposes.
